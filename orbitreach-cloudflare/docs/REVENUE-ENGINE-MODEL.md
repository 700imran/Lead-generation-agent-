# OrbitReach Revenue-Engine Model

## Client lifecycle

1. Research the business and its audience.
2. Build the website/app.
3. Add evidence-backed psychology and UI/UX decisions.
4. Run PageSpeed, accessibility, SEO, AEO and GEO audits.
5. Developer embeds the Website → Revenue Engine Connector.
6. QA verifies the connector and lead flow.
7. Admin approves activation.
8. Provision a tenant-isolated revenue-engine instance.
9. The instance continuously captures, enriches, qualifies and routes leads according to the client's approved configuration.
10. Sales agent drafts/sends outreach only within the configured approval policy.
11. Operations reports pipeline, conversions, response rates and service status.

## Commercial separation

- Website creation: one-time project fee.
- Hosting: recurring infrastructure/service fee.
- Maintenance: recurring support/update fee.
- Revenue Engine: separate recurring lead-generation and selling service.

The same client can therefore have a website subscription and a separate revenue-engine subscription. A future customizer can configure the revenue engine for industry, offer, geography, channels, qualification rules, messaging and sales workflow without changing the core Hermes control plane.

## Human psychology and UX boundary

Psychology research informs hypotheses and friction reduction. UI/UX decides the actual interface/components using those reports plus accessibility, business and performance evidence. No sensitive-trait inference or deceptive dark patterns.
