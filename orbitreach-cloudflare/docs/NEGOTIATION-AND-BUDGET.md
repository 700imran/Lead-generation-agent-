# Negotiation, Pricing & Budget Rules

## Demo progression
1. Low-commitment concept/demo A.
2. Low-commitment concept/demo B.
3. Exact-fit demo based on the client's stated business goals, brand, requirements and approved budget.

The low-value demos are for discovery and expectation calibration, not deliberate underperformance or deception.

## Budget principle
Client budget is a scope/feature/implementation constraint, not permission to ship a poor-quality or intentionally crippled website. Within the approved budget, preserve visual quality, usability, accessibility, performance and the client's primary conversion goal.

## Commercial authority
Agents may research, calculate and recommend. Admin approval is required to accept budget, change scope after quote, change payment terms, confirm payment received, or start paid delivery.

## Payment gate
`payment_requested -> admin_verification -> payment_verified -> delivery_start`.
The agent must never mark payment as received based only on a screenshot, webhook, email, or client statement unless the configured Admin/payment verification authority confirms it.

## Recurring model
- Website creation fee
- Hosting fee
- Maintenance fee
- Separate lead-generation/sales revenue-engine subscription
